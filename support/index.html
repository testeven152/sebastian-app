Get in touch

For help, bug reports or feature requests, email stevenkhangte@gmail.com, or use Settings › About › Send feedback in the app, which fills in your app version for you.

You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

The watch app doesn't show my class.

Make sure Sebastian is installed on both devices, open it on your iPhone with the watch nearby and unlocked, and start the class again.

Why does the watch ask for Health access?

It runs your class as a workout so the timer keeps going with your wrist down. Sebastian doesn't read your health data or save anything to Health.

Where are my routines stored?

On your iPhone, and in your own private iCloud when iCloud sync is on, so they appear on your other devices. The developer can't see them. See the privacy policy.
