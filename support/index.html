You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

The watch app doesn't show my class. Make sure Sebastian is installed on both devices, open it on your iPhone with the watch nearby and unlocked, and start the class again.

Why does the watch ask for Health access? It runs your class as a workout so the timer keeps going with your wrist down. Sebastian doesn't read your health data or save anything to Health.

Where are my routines stored? On your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
