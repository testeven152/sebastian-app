Privacy policy

Sebastian for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated 4 November 2026

Sebastian doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your routines and moves

The routines, moves and machines you create are stored on your device. The developer never receives them and can't see them.

Settings

Your preferences, such as sounds, haptics and appearance, are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch and never leave them.

Apple Watch and Health

The watch app receives your class from your own iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity link between the two devices. While a class runs, the watch starts a workout session so the timer and wrist taps keep going with the screen down. Sebastian doesn't read any of your health data, and it doesn't save workouts or anything else to the Health app.

Live Activities

During a class, the timer can be shown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This is drawn on your iPhone from your routine and isn't sent anywhere.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, your mail app opens a message to the developer. The developer receives only what you send in that email, which includes the app's version number in the subject line, and uses it only to reply and to improve the app.

Children

Sebastian doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version syncs your routines through your own iCloud account, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the support page.
