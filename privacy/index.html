Sebastian for iPhone and Apple Watch · Last updated 15 November 2026 (backup copy on your iPhone)

Privacy policy

Sebastian doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your routines and moves

The routines, moves and machines you create are stored on your iPhone.

When iCloud sync is available and you're signed in to iCloud with iCloud turned on for Sebastian, they're also stored in your own private iCloud database, so they appear on your other devices. Apple stores this data under your Apple Account and its privacy policy. The developer has no access to your private iCloud data and never sees your routines.

To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for Sebastian in the Settings app. iOS then removes the synced routines from Sebastian on that iPhone; they stay in your iCloud and come back if you turn it on again. Deleting a routine in Sebastian deletes it from your devices and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device, including the backup copy below. To remove the iCloud copy as well, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Backup copy on your iPhone

Sebastian also keeps a copy of your routines, moves and machines in its own storage on your iPhone, so you can restore them if iCloud stops syncing them, for example after you turn off iCloud for Sebastian or sign out. The copy isn't sent anywhere by Sebastian; like the rest of the app's data, it's included in your iPhone's own backups.

So that the copy is only ever restored for the person it belongs to, it's marked with a one-way code made from your iCloud account's identifier. The code can't be turned back into your account details. If a different Apple Account signs in to iCloud on the iPhone, the copy is deleted.

Settings

Your preferences, such as sounds, haptics and appearance, are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch and never leave them.

Apple Watch and Health

The watch app receives your class from your own iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity link between the two devices. While a class runs, the watch starts a workout session so the timer and wrist taps keep going with the screen down. Sebastian doesn't read any of your health data, and it doesn't save workouts or anything else to the Health app.

Live Activities

During a class, the timer can be shown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. This is drawn on your iPhone from your routine and isn't sent anywhere.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, your mail app opens a message to the developer. The developer receives only what you send in that email, which includes the app's version number in the subject line, and uses it only to reply and to improve the app.

Children

Sebastian doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
